Six dimensions of the toolkit

  1. 01 / Skill

    Judgement, packaged.

    An agent skill that reads your project before touching it — framework, design system, existing libraries — then commits to a direction and the smallest change that achieves it.

    skills/snowbros-creative · detect-stack · audit-motion

  2. 02 / Motion

    One clock for every frame.

    Timelines, springs, scroll progress and sticky pinning share a single frame loop that sleeps when nothing moves. Every pattern ships a designed reduced-motion alternative.

    @snowbros/core · @snowbros/motion

  3. 03 / Interaction

    Controls that answer back.

    Magnetic targets, tilt, spotlight and spring feedback — with pointer, touch and keyboard treated as equals, never as afterthoughts.

    @snowbros/fx · springs from @snowbros/core

  4. 04 / Space

    3D that knows when to stop.

    Managed three.js stages render on demand, cap pixel ratio by device tier, fall back to SVG, and hand their GPU context back when they leave. This crystal is one.

    @snowbros/scene · three (optional peer)

  5. 05 / Effects

    Texture without assets.

    Procedural fields, masked reveals, scramble and path drawing — generated in code, so they scale, theme and respect preferences.

    @snowbros/fx

  6. 06 / Interface

    Calm where work happens.

    Functional motion for dashboards, tables and forms: short, interruptible, explaining what changed and nothing more. Adapters for React and Web Components.

    @snowbros/react · @snowbros/elements · @snowbros/tokens

Six dimensions, one lattice. Every arm is a package you can adopt on its own.

02 — Thesis

Thesis

Most motion on the web is decoration. We build motion that explains — what changed, where it went, what to do next. One clock drives every frame. Native scroll stays native. Reduced motion is a design, not a switch. And nothing ships until it has been measured.

Causality
Every movement shows what caused it and where things went.
Frequency
The more often an action happens, the less it should move.
Interruptible
Nobody waits for an animation. Scroll, click and keys always win.
Measured
Budgets before visuals. Frame times, not adjectives.

B Interaction lab

Feel the difference.

Three primitives, each usable by pointer, touch and keyboard. On touch screens the pointer-only effects step aside instead of misfiring.

magnetic · tilt · spotlight · createSpringValue

magnetic() The target leans toward a fine pointer within 80 px, then springs home. 0 activations

Tilt with the pointer, or use the arrow keys while focused. Escape levels it.

tilt() + spotlight() Perspective tilt and a light that follows the pointer. Keyboard: focus the pass, use arrow keys.

Press and hold with pointer, Space or Enter for one second.

createSpringValue() Hold for one second — release early and the spring rewinds. Works with Space and Enter.
